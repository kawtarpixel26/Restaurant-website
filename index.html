Our Heritage & Craft

Forged By Flame, Rooted In Agadir

Located at 2 Rue des Orangers in the vibrant heart of Agadir, Blacksmith was created to celebrate the raw power of charcoal grilling and the warmth of Moroccan dining. We fuse premium butcher cuts and artisanal smokehouse recipes with regional Atlantic delicacies and traditional spices.

With over 3,940 verified reviews and an outstanding 4.7-star rating, our kitchen is renowned for generous portions, friendly late-night service (open daily until 1:00 AM), and great value ranging from MAD 100 to 200 per guest.
