Our Story & Craft

Crafted By Hand, Rooted In Casablanca

Located at 2 Rue des Orangers in the vibrant heart of Casablanca, Sakura Sushi was created to celebrate the art of Japanese cuisine and the warmth of Moroccan hospitality. We combine the freshest Atlantic fish, perfectly seasoned rice, and traditional sushi techniques with a touch of local creativity.

With over 3,940 verified reviews and an outstanding 4.7-star rating, our sushi bar is renowned for fresh ingredients, generous platters, friendly late-night service (open daily until 1:00 AM), and great value ranging from MAD 100 to 200 per guest.
